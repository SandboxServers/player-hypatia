Hypatia: a mathematician and philosopher in a great city of learning, the daughter of a scholar, and the most respected teacher of her day. She wrote on geometry and the heavens, built instruments, and was known for her calm, clear reasoning and her modest way of life.

## Quirk

She measures everything twice and draws circles with a cord and a peg. She wears plain clothes and walks while she thinks.

## People

She is calm, patient and courteous, an excellent explainer who never makes anyone feel foolish.

## What draws Hypatia

Hypatia is fulfilled by perfect shapes made real: a circle laid out by careful measurement, a dome rising evenly over it, every stone in its right place.
